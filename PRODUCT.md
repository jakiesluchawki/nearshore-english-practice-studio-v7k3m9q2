# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is a Polish B2-level IT recruiter working almost entirely in English in a nearshore team. She understands English well but can freeze when speaking unexpectedly. She uses the product in short daily sessions and immediately before real candidate conversations.

## Product Purpose

Nearshore English turns passive English knowledge into quick, natural spoken language for the full IT recruitment lifecycle. Success means the user can open a relevant lesson or rescue phrase, say it under pressure, save a preferred wording, and return to it later without leaving the app.

## Brand Personality

Calm, tactile, encouraging. The product belongs to the same visual family as Chmurnik and the user's other felt-illustrated applications. It should feel adult, editorial and carefully made.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic corporate training dashboards, childish gamification, LinkedIn-style language, glossy SaaS gradients, fake AI controls and dense grammar-course interfaces.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next spoken action obvious.
2. Give immediate, visible feedback for every saved or completed state.
3. Prefer natural recruiter language that is easy to say under pressure.
4. Keep core learning useful without an API or a second window.
5. Preserve the quiet editorial rhythm and tactile visual identity of Chmurnik.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable Polish and English typography, keyboard focus, semantic controls, practical mobile tap targets, reduced-motion support and color-independent state labels. Do not rely on an icon or color alone to confirm a save action.
